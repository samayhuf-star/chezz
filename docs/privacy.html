Chesz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Chesz ("the app") is published by Samay Vashsht. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Profile: you play as a guest, with Sign in with Apple, or with an email account. A profile stores the display name, city, state and country you type in, plus your game statistics (points, wins, draws, losses, levels beaten, streaks, average move time, recent results). Display name, location and points appear on the public leaderboards. Do not enter personal information you do not want shown publicly.
  • Email account (optional): if you create an account we store your email address and a salted, hashed password (we never store or see your password in plain text). Your email is never shown to other players and is used only to sign you in.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): we receive an anonymous Apple account identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name. We do not receive your Apple password.
  • Live games: when you play another player we store the moves and result of that game, and your display name, country and online rating are shown to your opponent.
  • Push notifications (optional): if you allow notifications, our notification provider OneSignal receives a device push token, your anonymous player ID, your city and country, and basic device information (model, system version, language, time zone) so that we can send you game and season notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Reports: if you report a player's name, we store which player you reported so we can review it.
  • Ranked games: to verify a ranked result we receive the list of moves of that game and a random game identifier.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play and by our subscription provider RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status. We never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contact list, precise location, photos, advertising identifiers, or any data for tracking or advertising. The app contains no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.

On-device data

Your settings and any unfinished game are stored on your device. Your profile secret is kept in the device's secure storage.

Data retention and deletion

Profile, account and statistics are kept while your profile exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: open Profile, then Settings, then "Delete account". Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also email samayhuf@gmail.com from any address and quote your display name, and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
