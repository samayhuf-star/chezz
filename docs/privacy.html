Chezz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Chezz ("the app") is published by Samay Vashsht. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Optional profile: if you create a profile, we store the display name, city, state and country you type in, plus your game statistics (points, wins, draws, losses, levels beaten). These appear on the public leaderboards. Do not enter personal information you do not want shown publicly.
  • Ranked games: to verify a ranked result we receive the list of moves of that game and a random game identifier.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play and by our subscription provider RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status. We never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contact list, precise location, photos, advertising identifiers, or any data for tracking or advertising. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device data

Your settings and any unfinished game are stored on your device. Your profile secret is kept in the device's secure storage.

Data retention and deletion

Profile and statistics are kept while your profile exists. To delete your profile and data, email samayhuf@gmail.com from any address and quote your display name; we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
