**Description:**
Chesz turns chess into a league your whole city plays in. Every ranked point you win is added to your city's total, and every month the cities start again from zero.

CITY VS CITY SEASONS
• A new season every calendar month: cities are ranked by the points their players win
• Bot wins, live wins and live draws all count for your city
• See your city's rank, how many of its players are active, and how much you added

LIVE GAMES AGAINST REAL PLAYERS
• 7-minute games with live matchmaking and an online rating
• Rematch, draw offers and a full game review afterwards

50 BOTS, 5 TIERS
• From a friendly beginner to Grandmaster Zero: Beginner, Casual, Club, Advanced, Master
• Bots run on the Stockfish engine and answer in about a second
• Win = Level × 10 points. Draw = Level × 4. Ranked results are re-checked by our server

LEAGUE TABLES AND FAIR PLAY
• Player boards for your city, state, country and the world, by bot points or online rating
• Report or hide any player name you do not want to see
• Real clocks, confirm before resigning, automatic draws, resume after interruptions

MORE TO DO
• A daily puzzle, 223 mates in 1 and 2, and a play streak
• Board themes, light and dark mode
• Sign in with Apple, email, or play bots as a guest

Bot levels 1–10 and 5 live games a day are free. Chesz Pro unlocks levels 11–50, unlimited hints and unlimited live games.
