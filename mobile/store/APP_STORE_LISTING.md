**Description:**
Chesz turns chess into a league you can actually climb. Beat bots, earn points, and see where you stand in your city, your state, your country and the world.

YOUR CITY, YOUR LEAGUE
• Four league tables: city, state, country and worldwide
• Every ranked result is verified by our server, so every rank is earned
• Report or hide any player name you do not want to see

50 BOTS, 5 TIERS
• From a friendly beginner to Grandmaster Zero: Beginner, Casual, Club, Advanced, Master
• Each bot answers in about a second, so the clock stays real
• Win = Level × 10 points. Draw = Level × 4

REAL CLOCKS, FAIR PLAY
• 7 minutes each side. Clocks start after both first moves
• Confirm before resigning, illegal-move hints, draw offers, automatic draws, resume after interruptions
• Step back through any game and share it as PGN

MORE TO DO
• Daily puzzles and a play streak
• Board themes and piece sets
• Your player card with rating and levels beaten
